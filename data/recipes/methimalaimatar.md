## Methi Malai Matar



### Ingredients

For 2–3 people:

- Coconut oil
- 4 cloves
- 1-inch piece of cinnamon
- 3 green cardamom pods
- 1 tsp Jeera (cumin seeds)
- 1 green chili
- 3 cloves of garlic
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 1 zucchini, peeled or unpeeled and cut | or 1 onion
- 20–30 cashews
- 250 ml coconut milk
- fresh methi leaves (or 1 tbsp kasuri methi)
- frozen peas (half a packet, ~200g?)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- Crushed black pepper, to taste
- Salt, to taste

Note: replace zucchini with onion

### Steps

(Pavi's version)

1. Heat coconut oil in a pot. Add 4 cloves, 1 inch cinnamon, 3 green cardomom and jeera. Add two green chillies, 3 cloves of garlic, an inch of grated ginger. 
You could add an onion but I didn't.

2. Add 1 cut zucchini (I peeled the skin cos I wanted to the gravy to resemble the color of cream, but you can add it with the skin too for additional nutrition.)

3. When the zucchini softens, add some cashews (20-30), add 250ml of coconut milk (I used the one with 18% fat) turn off the burner and wait for it to cool for sometime.

4. In the meantime prepare the fresh methi leaves - pluck from the stem and run it under water.

5. Transfer the contents of the pot to a blender and grind until it becomes smooth paste.

6. Add coconut oil to the pot again and saute the methi leaves for a few minutes.

7. Add the ground cream, frozen peas, a teaspoon of garam masala, salt, crushed pepper (very important), crushed dried methi leaves and turn off the hob.
