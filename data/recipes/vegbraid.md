## Veg braid



### Ingredients

For 4 people:

- Vegan minced meat 
- 1 onion
- Parsley 
- Sage
- Paprika powder
- Thyme
- Italian pasta seasoning
- Nutmeg
- Salt and Pepper
- Dried tomatoes
- Cheese
- Puff pastry


### Steps

1. Preheat oven to 180°C 
2. Chop onions finely and fry them in some olive oil with chopped parsley. 
3. Add the vegan minced meat and add some more olive oil if too dry.
4. While cooking for about 5 to 10 minutes, season with all the herbs, salt and pepper.  Be generous with sage.
5. Keep aside and spread out a sheet of puff pastry on a tray.
6. Spread the cooked filling and garnish with dried tomatoes,  some additional parsley and cheese.
7. Slit the sides of the pastry diagonally into several strips. Fold over the middle over the filling in alternating folds to form the plait.
8. Optionally baste with an egg yolk for golden crust.
9. Bake for 20 to 30 min 
10. Serve with fresh salad
